Disclosure

Method and limits

This is a reference-grounded artistic reconstruction, not survey data, a flight simulator, or a navigational product.

World scale
1 Three.js unit = 1 metre. +X east, +Y up, +Z south.
Geology
Connected valleys and varied hoodoos follow published erosion, spacing, slope, and tuff-layer constraints. Forms are deterministic procedural interpretations, not scans of named sites.
Rock-cut spaces
Openings have extruded stone rims and recessed tunnel volumes. They evoke regional construction by removal, but do not reproduce a specific church or dwelling.
Sun
Authored dawn at 108.5° azimuth and 5.8° elevation. Sky, visible disc, direct light, HDR environment and shadows share one direction. Values are illustrative, not a date-specific ephemeris.
Balloons
Vertical motion uses a conservative buoyancy/drag approximation with thermostatic burner control; horizontal advection follows a layered ENE wind. Pilot procedures, wake turbulence and full envelope thermodynamics are not simulated.
Assets
No mutable runtime assets or remote calls. All visible geometry and textures are generated locally in code; the visual benchmark is documentation-only.
