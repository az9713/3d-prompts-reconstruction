Modern astronomical approximation

Built for the living site

Position: 51.1831565° N, 1.8588747° W (English Heritage visitor-site coordinate). Year: 2026.

Solstice presets use the NOAA/Meeus solar equations and the standard sunrise/sunset apparent altitude of −0.833° (solar radius plus nominal refraction); the local horizon is modeled as flat. The displayed shadow is a geometric projection from the outer sarsen uprights and lintels.

Atmosphere, terrain, exact stone survey coordinates, historic horizon, and archaeological sight-lines are not modeled. This is not an archaeological-survey reconstruction.

Sources: English Heritage, “Stonehenge Directions”; NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory, “Sunrise/Sunset Calculations.”
